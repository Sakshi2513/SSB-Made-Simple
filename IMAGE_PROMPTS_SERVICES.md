# Prompts for the "Three Services" cards

Save in `public/services/` as `army.webp`, `navy.webp`, `air-force.webp` (.jpg or .png also work). Size: 1200 x 1500 px (4:5 portrait). Keep the officer in the upper-middle of the frame, because the name and motto sit over the bottom of the card.

**Style prefix (paste before each):**
> Photorealistic cinematic portrait photograph, 4:5 portrait, dramatic golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, ultra-detailed, the officer looking confidently into the distance, subject in the upper-middle of the frame, dark empty space in the lower third. No text, no logos, no watermark.

**Negative prompt:** `text, letters, watermark, logo, cartoon, illustration, distorted face, extra fingers, low quality, blur`

| File | Subject |
|---|---|
| `army.webp` | A young Indian Army officer in olive green combat uniform and beret standing on a rugged mountain ridge at sunrise, snow-capped peaks and layered mist behind him. |
| `navy.webp` | A young Indian Navy officer in crisp white uniform and white cap standing on the deck of a warship at dusk, calm sea and an orange horizon behind him, the ship's rail in the foreground. |
| `air-force.webp` | A young Indian Air Force officer in a blue uniform and peaked cap standing on an airfield at sunrise, a fighter jet parked behind her, clouds lit gold and a faint tricolour contrail in the sky. |
